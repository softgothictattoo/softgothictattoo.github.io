Booking

Book online on Venue.ink Message me on Instagram

What to include

  • Your idea, or the flash design you've seen
  • Where on the body
  • Rough size in centimetres, or compared to something (palm, phone, pineapple, etc.)
  • Any references or examples you like, my work or otherwise
  • Roughly when you're free

Deposits and cancellations

A deposit of £50 secures your appointment and comes off the final price. Rescheduling with at least 48 hours' notice moves the deposit to your new date; less than that and it's kept to cover the lost slot.

Pricing

Small designs start from £50. Custom work is quoted per design, once the scale and detail of work is established.

Before you come

  • Eat properly and bring water
  • If possible, wear something that gives easy access to the area
  • You should avoid drinking alcohol the night before
  • Avoid sunburn on selected area as it cannot be tattooed over
  • Bring photo ID, 18+ only.

Aftercare

You'll leave with written aftercare and a wrap. Keep it clean, keep it moisturised with a thin layer, no soaking, no sun, no picking. Message me if anything looks off.
