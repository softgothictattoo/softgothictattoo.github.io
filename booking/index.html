Booking

Book online on Venue.ink Message me on Instagram

What to include

  • Your idea, or selected flash design
  • Desired placement on your body
  • Rough size in centimetres, or compared to something (palm, phone, pineapple, etc.)
  • Any references or examples you like, my work or otherwise
  • Your upcoming availability and preferred times of the week

Deposits and cancellations

A deposit of £50 secures your appointment and comes off the final price. Rescheduling with at least 48 hours' notice moves the deposit to your new date; less than that and it's kept to cover the lost slot.

Pricing

Small designs start from £50. Custom work is quoted per design, once the scale and detail of work is established. Estimate price ranges for flash are available on Venue, however if anything doesn't seem right, please get in contact.

Before your appointment

  • Make sure you have eaten before your appointment
  • Bring any snacks and drinks
  • If possible, wear something that gives easy access to the area
  • You should avoid drinking alcohol the night before
  • Avoid sunburn on selected area as it cannot be tattooed over
  • Bring photo ID, 18+ only

Aftercare

You will be walked through the aftercare during the appointment. Should you have any questions or concerns beforehand or following your appointment, please send me a message
