About Luke

I'm a tattoo artist based in Bristol, working from Studio Trinket in the old city. I've been tattooing for about two years, and drawing for a lot longer.

Gothic things, drawn gently. Daggers, moths, saints, thorns. Clean lines and soft shading rather than heavy outlines. Every piece is drawn for the body it's going on.

How I work

Custom pieces start with a message: your idea, where it's going, rough size, and any references. I'll draw it up before your appointment and we'll adjust it together on the day. Flash is ready to go and usually cheaper.

Everyone is welcome

All skin tones, all bodies, all genders, all ages over 18. I work with the skin in front of me — design and contrast are planned so the piece reads well on you, not on a stock photo. If you have questions about how something will heal on your skin, ask; it's a normal conversation, not an awkward one.

The studio is a safe, private space. If anything would make your session easier — a break, a friend with you, a quieter time of day — say so when you book.
